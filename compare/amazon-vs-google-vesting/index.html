Comparison FAQs

Why does Amazon use 5/15/40/40 vesting?

Amazon backloads equity into Years 3 and 4 and compensates in Years 1 and 2 with cash signing bonuses, assuming 15% annual stock appreciation in internal total comp targets.

What happens if I leave Amazon before Year 3?

You only vest 20% of your initial equity grant after 2 full years (vs. 50% at Google or Meta), making upfront cash sign-on negotiation critical.
