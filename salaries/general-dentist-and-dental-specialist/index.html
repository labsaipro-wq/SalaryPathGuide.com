Scope, Competencies & Promotion Criteria

Associate Dentist (Entry / GPR / AEGD Resident)

Operates under senior clinical guidance; delivers comprehensive exams, posterior composites, single-unit crowns, and basic non-surgical extractions.

Experienced Associate Dentist

Independently runs 2-3 operatory columns; executes multi-unit prosthetics, molar endodontics, surgical extractions with socket preservation, and aligner therapy.

Molar Rotary EndodonticsSurgical Extractions & Bone GraftingClear Aligner OrthodonticsCone Beam CT (CBCT) 3D DiagnosisHigh-Acceptance Case PresentationOral Conscious Sedation

Senior Associate / Lead Clinical Dentist

Dental Practice Owner / Managing Partner

Owns and operates a private practice or multi-location dental group; oversees hygiene schedules, associate compensation, P&L, and patient acquisition.
