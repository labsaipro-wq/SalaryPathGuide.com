Scope, Competencies & Promotion Criteria

L3 · 0 - 2 Years Median Base: $92,000

Associate Product Manager (APM)

Drafts PRDs for small features, tracks sprint backlog, analyzes user feedback and telemetry metrics.

Product Analytics (Mixpanel/Amplitude)Agile Sprint GroomingUser InterviewingSQL for PMsWireframing
L4 · 2 - 5 Years Median Base: $135,000

Product Manager (PM II)

Owns a full product pod or feature suite; formulates quarterly roadmap, balances user delight with business revenue.

Roadmap Prioritization (RICE)A/B ExperimentationGo-To-Market (GTM) StrategyCompetitive IntelligenceStakeholder Alignment
L5 · 5 - 8 Years Median Base: $185,000

Senior Product Manager

Drives multi-product vision, orchestrates multi-quarter strategic bets, aligns sales, design, and engineering.

Strategic VisionExecutive StorytellingMonetization & Pricing ModelsPlatform ThinkingTalent Mentorship
L6 · 8 - 12+ Years Median Base: $250,000

Principal PM / Group Product Manager (GPM)

Leads a portfolio of product pods or manages a team of 4-8 PMs; shapes annual budget and multi-year product strategy.

Org Design & Team BuildingCorporate StrategyM&A IntegrationHigh-Stakes Partner Negotiation
L7 · 12+ Years Median Base: $335,000

Director of Product / VP Product

Accountable for company product portfolio success, investor updates, cross-functional organization leadership.

Executive LeadershipMarket PositioningOrganizational ScalingBoard Advisory
