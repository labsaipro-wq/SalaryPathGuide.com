Key Questions Answered

What is a Year 2 sign-on cliff?

When a large Year 1 signing bonus expires without an offsetting equity vest or base raise in Year 2, total annual compensation drops sharply.

How do I negotiate around a sign-on cliff?

Request a Year 2 cash bridge, an increased initial equity grant, or a written target annual refresher commitment.
