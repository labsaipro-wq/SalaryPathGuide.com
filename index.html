Strategic Compensation Playbook

Salary Negotiation Playbook & Scripts

Verbatim phone and email scripts tested across hundreds of tech offers. Customize placeholders with your targets and negotiate with confidence.

01

Never Accept in Real-Time

Always request 48-72 hours to review the full written documentation. Verbal acceptance on the phone strips your bargaining leverage immediately.

02

Negotiate Total Comp, Not Just Base

If base pay is restricted by HR bands, pivot immediately to sign-on bonuses, equity refreshes, relocation budgets, or accelerated 6-month review cadences.

03

Anchor to Objective Benchmarks

Frame counter-offers around data percentiles (e.g. 75th percentile for your level) and concrete business impact rather than personal cost-of-living increases.

04

Keep Collaboration at the Center

Treat the recruiter as an internal ally. Frame negotiations as joint problem-solving: "I want to sign today—help me get this over the finish line."
